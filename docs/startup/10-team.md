# 10 — Team

## Founder

Solo technical founder (India-based). Profile:

* **Builder**: shipped the entire Lumen product alone — full-stack (Next.js/TypeScript/Express/Prisma/Postgres), AI pipeline (RAG, local embeddings, extraction), Python parsing sidecar, infra, security hardening — via AI-assisted development at a speed and cost basis no funded team matches.
* **Network**: warm access to Dubai/Middle East, UK, and USA — precisely the three regions where the target buyers live. The diaspora is dense in DIFC's fund world, London desks, and NY credit funds: warm-intro territory where competitors face cold outreach.
* **Cost discipline**: ~$0/month burn; product live in production. Infinite build runway.
* **Gaps to be honest about**: enterprise sales experience; finance-domain credibility (mitigated by advisors, below); doing build + sell + support solo (mitigated by ruthless scope — the non-goals list in [06](06-roadmap.md)).

## Advisors to recruit (equity 0.25–1% each)

1. **A credit-fund COO or head of portfolio ops** (US or UK) — the exact buyer. Advises on covenant workflows, opens pilot doors, validates the roadmap. Highest-leverage hire in the company's first year.
2. **A GCC family-office insider** (Dubai) — credibility + introductions in DIFC; part of the Dubai wedge.
3. **A security/compliance advisor** — guide SOC2 efficiently; ideally someone funds already trust.

## Hiring plan (revenue-triggered, not time-triggered)

| Trigger | Hire | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~$250K ARR, or support interrupts building | Contractor: ops/support (part-time) | Free the founder to build + sell |
| 10+ customers | First full-time: founder-facing sales/CS hybrid | Founder stays on product; repeatable pipeline runs |
| 20+ customers | Engineer #2 | Integration requests, private-deployment tier |
| SOC2 Type II window | Compliance lead (fractional) | Evidence discipline without a full-time FTE |

Until then: deliberately solo. A one-person 90%-margin company at 30 customers is a strong outcome on its own, and a stronger negotiating position with any acquirer.

## Culture (write it down early, it compounds)

* **Grounded, not guessed** — internally too: no unverifiable claims in docs, decks, or demos.
* **The lineage discipline** — every number (product) and every commitment (business) traceable to a source.
* **Small, fast, honest** — the cost asymmetry is a strategic weapon; don't copy enterprise bloat.
