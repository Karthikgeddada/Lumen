# 04 — Go-to-Market

## Beachhead

**Mid-market private credit funds ($500M–5B AUM, 5–30 people), US + UK first; GCC family offices as the parallel Dubai-network wedge.** One buyer persona: the fund's COO / head of portfolio ops / senior analyst who personally assembles the covenant tracker every quarter.

## The sale in one line

"Send us one real borrower package under NDA. In 24 hours you'll have your covenant dashboard — every number traced to its page. If your analyst verifies two numbers against the PDF, you'll know this works."

Nobody in this market buys slides; they buy **their own documents processed**. The 24-hour demo IS the go-to-market.

## Sales motion (per prospect)

1. **Warm intro / direct outreach** (see Channels) → 20-min call: ask how they handle quarterly packages today. Do NOT pitch first. Listen for: "we have an analyst who…", "it's a mess every quarter", "we paste into ChatGPT but…"
2. **The ask**: one real borrower package under mutual NDA (their paper or ours — simple mutual NDA template).
3. **24-hour turnaround**: entity dashboard, 4–8 quarters of metrics with lineage, covenant pass/fail, 3 cited answers.
4. **The verification moment**: their analyst checks two numbers against the source PDF. This is the conversion point — design the demo so verification is effortless.
5. **Pilot**: 90-day paid pilot (paid = commitment; free pilots churn), 1–3 borrowers, weekly check-in.
6. **Expand**: more borrowers → more workspaces → scheduled quarterly reports → renewal at standard tier.

## Channels by region

| Region | Channel | Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai/GCC | Diaspora network (DIFC analysts, COOs, fund managers — heavily Indian-origin) | Warm intros + live demo sessions; relationship-led; consider a local partner/advisor for credibility |
| UK | Direct outreach + niche communities (private credit LinkedIn groups, LMA events) | 24-hour demo offer in first message; target 20 prospects |
| USA | Direct outreach (LinkedIn/email) + credit-fund podcasts/newsletters for awareness | Volume play: 50 prospects, same demo offer; collect references early |

## Diaspora advantage (deliberate GTM asset)

Dubai's fund world runs on the Indian diaspora; London and NY desks are the same. For a solo Indian founder, that's warm-intro territory where a Silicon Valley startup faces cold outreach. **Treat the network as a channel and work it systematically**: list 50 named people across the three regions, tier them by warmth, sequence outreach.

## Pilot plan (the number that matters first)

* **3 paid pilots in 90 days** — sourced across the three regions; ~$10–15K total pilot revenue; the goal is testimonials + 2 case-study-grade wins, not revenue.
* **10 paying customers by month 6–9.**
* **25–30 by month 18–24 ≈ $1M ARR.**

## Messaging pillars

1. **Grounded, not guessed** — every number shows its source page and derivation; humans confirm before it's official.
2. **Covenant monitoring, automated** — the quarterly package becomes the quarterly dashboard without re-keying.
3. **Your data stays yours** — private AI path, no training on customer data (must be TRUE before use — see [08](08-security.md)).
4. **Live in a day, priced for your size** — vs. enterprise suites built for $10B funds.

## What NOT to do

* Don't sell "AI document chat" — undifferentiated, invites ChatGPT comparison.
* Don't chase hedge funds first — Hebbia owns that segment; our wedge is monitoring, not research.
* Don't run free pilots — unpaid pilots get unpaid attention.
* Don't build for enterprise procurement (SSO/SAML, custom paper) before ~10 customers ask.

## Metrics from day one

Demos run / packages received / 24h-turnaround rate / verification-pass rate / pilot conversion / logo churn / NRR. Instrument the product now (usage events per workspace) so pilots generate data, not anecdotes.
